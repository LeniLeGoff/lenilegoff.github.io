Leni K. Le Goff is currently holding a position at Edinburgh Napier University as Lecturer in AI and Machine Learning.

During LKL's academic career, I had the opportunity to work in several projects in the fields of Developmental Robotics and Evolutionary Robotics, two complementary fields studying embodied intelligence. 

My [Ph.D.](https://theses.hal.science/tel-02101369/) was funded by an EU-funded project [DREAM](https://dream.isir.upmc.fr) (2015-2018). 
For this project, I studied the autonomous grounding of a robot's perception of its surrounding through manipulation and image processing. I developed a novel classifier called [collaborative mixture models](https://www.github.com/LeniLeGoff/CMM_Lib) able to handle uncertainty, to learn on a stream of data, and with a low number of samples. 

Then, I obtained a position in the field of Evolutionary Robotics as a Research Fellow in the EPSRC-funded ARE project (2019-2022). The project was about generative design of robots and the challenging task of learning controllers for robots with unknown designs. I developed a new sample-efficient machine learning algorithm based on evolutionary strategies and probabilistic distribution estimation, called NIP-ES and integrated it into an efficient algorithm to generate diverse robot designs for a target task called MELAI. Moreover, I developed and still maintain the open-source framework of the [ARE project](https://www.github.com/AutonomousRoboticEvolution/AREFramework).
